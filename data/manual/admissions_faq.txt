Q: What courses does SIES (Nerul) offer at the undergraduate level?
A: SIES (Nerul) College offers undergraduate programs including B.Com,
B.Com (Accounting & Finance), B.Com (Banking & Insurance), B.Com (Financial
Markets), B.Com (Management Accounting with Finance), B.Com
(Entrepreneurship), B.M.S. (Bachelor of Management Studies), B.A.M.M.C.
(Bachelor of Arts in Multimedia and Mass Communication), B.Sc. (Computer
Science), B.Sc. (Information Technology), B.Sc. (Environmental Science),
B.Sc. (Data Science), B.Sc. (Artificial Intelligence), and B.Sc. (Packaging
Technology). Junior College (FYJC) admissions for Class 11 are also offered
in Arts, Commerce, and Science streams.

Q: What courses does SIES (Nerul) offer at the postgraduate level?
A: Postgraduate programs include M.Com (Advanced Accountancy), M.Com
(Business Management), M.A. (Business Economics), M.A. (Multimedia and Mass
Communication), M.Sc. (Computer Science), M.Sc. (Information Technology),
and M.Sc. (Environmental Science).

Q: How is admission to undergraduate degree courses decided?
A: Admission to UG courses is merit-based, calculated from Class 12 (HSC)
board exam marks. There is generally no separate entrance exam, except for
B.M.S., which considers MHT-CET scores in addition to merit.

Q: What is the admission process for degree courses?
A: Students first register on the University of Mumbai's centralized
admission portal (Samarth), then also fill out the college's own admission
form. The college publishes merit lists in multiple rounds based on Class 12
marks; students on the merit list are called for document verification and
fee payment to confirm their seat.

Q: What is the FYJC (Class 11) admission process?
A: FYJC admission is conducted through Maharashtra's centralized online FYJC
admission portal, common across Mumbai and other major cities in the state.
Students register on the portal, fill in their Class 10 (SSC) marks and
preferences, and are allotted seats through multiple merit-based CAP
(Centralized Admission Process) rounds.

Q: What is the Tamil Linguistic Minority Quota?
A: SIES (Nerul) College has a linguistic minority status recognizing its
Tamil-speaking founding community. As a result, a significant portion of
seats (historically around 50% in several courses) are reserved under the
Tamil Linguistic Minority Quota, alongside general/open category seats.
Merit lists are published separately for the Tamil/OLM (Other Linguistic
Minority) quota and the general category.

Q: What documents are required at the time of admission?
A: Commonly required documents include: Transfer Certificate, Class 10/12
mark sheet, Aadhar Card, residence proof, passport-size photographs,
category/caste verification certificate (if applicable), migration
certificate (if applicable), and gap certificate (if there is a gap in
education). Exact document requirements are listed in each year's official
admission notice on the college website.

Q: What is the minimum eligibility for admission?
A: Eligibility varies by course. As a general guide: B.Sc. programs
typically require around 45% aggregate in Class 12; B.M.S. requires around
45% (40% for reserved categories); B.Com programs have varying cutoffs
depending on category and year. B.A.M.M.C. requires a pass in Class 12 from
a recognized board. Always check the current year's official cutoff, since
these change annually based on applicant numbers.

Q: Is there an entrance exam for admission?
A: Most UG courses do not require a separate entrance exam and are purely
merit-based on Class 12 marks. B.M.S. considers MHT-CET scores. Some
postgraduate courses (like M.Sc.) may require qualifying through MAH-CET.

Q: How can I check merit lists and admission notices?
A: Merit lists, cut-offs, and admission notices are published on the
official Admissions page: https://siesascn.edu.in/admissions
This page is updated frequently during the admission season with round-wise
merit lists for each course and quota category.

Q: Is there any donation or capitation fee for admission?
A: No. SIES (Nerul) College follows a strict no-donation admission policy.
Admission is purely merit-based, and students should not pay any money to
intermediaries or agents claiming to secure admission.

Q: How many students study at SIES (Nerul) College?
A: The college started in 1998 with 90 students and has grown to a strength
of approximately 4,700+ students across all its Junior College, UG, and PG
programs.
