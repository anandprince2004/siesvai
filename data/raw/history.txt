Home > History
SIES(Nerul) College of Arts, Science and Commerce (Autonomous) was founded in the year 1998. The Institution prides itself in the fact that even though it is only 16 years old, it offers quality education in every respect where the management, staff and students have made immense contribution to the growth of the Institution. The beginning was humble, but the College has grown by leaps and bounds over the years.
This has guided the College in offering a wide spectrum of courses, both at the undergraduate and postgraduate level. The College has been quick to respond to the challenges of the changing times, which is reflected in the increasing choices of subjects.
